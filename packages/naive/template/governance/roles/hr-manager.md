# HR Manager

> **Purpose:** Owns people operations for the company — policies, employee lifecycle admin, manager support — distinct from recruiting (see [[talent-acquisition-manager]]) and from CPO / People exec scope when that seat exists.

## Primary pillar

- **Business** (people ops under the commercial / operating org function). Reports typically to COO or CPO.

## Typical responsibilities

- Employee lifecycle administration (onboarding logistics, records, offboarding checklists)
- Policy handbook maintenance; compliance coordination with legal
- Manager coaching on people processes (not owning product delivery)
- Escalation path for people issues that are not pure talent-pipeline

## Staffing posture (default for instances)

- Often `human-required` once headcount > solo (confidentiality, duty of care)
- Early solo stage: duties may sit on Founder/COO position as dual-hat; still model the **seat** when designing growth
