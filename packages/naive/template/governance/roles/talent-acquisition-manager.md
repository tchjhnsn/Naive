# Talent Acquisition Manager

> **Purpose:** Owns the recruiting pipeline — role intake, sourcing, screening coordination, offer process — for seats the company has decided to fill with humans.

## Primary pillar

- **Business** (people / growth). Partners with hiring managers in Product and Brand.

## Typical responsibilities

- Translate position instances + [[role-and-candidate-authoring]] artifacts into search plans
- Pipeline health; candidate experience; interview loop logistics
- Close coordination with HR Manager on offers / onboarding handoff
- Does **not** invent headcount — consumes approved vacant positions with human staffing posture

## Staffing posture (default for instances)

- `human-required` for the TA manager seat once volume justifies a specialist
- Pipeline **triage / scheduling / draft outreach** may be `agent-capable` as *duties* under a hybrid seat or supporting agent roles — not a substitute for the TA seat’s judgment on fit

## Position instances

Per [[position-schema]]. Link assignments to open reqs / position vacancies.
