# Template: the pricing record

Fills `<venture>/purpose/validation/pricing.json`. Name the **buyer**: the person who pays may not be the user, and only the buyer's evidence counts as the target audience for pricing.

## Evidence, strongest first

1. Real payments at the price (behavior).
2. Deposits or pre-orders at the price (commitment).
3. Price sensitivity questions (stated), from van Westendorp's meter. Ask the buyer at what price the product would be:
   - so cheap they would doubt its quality;
   - a bargain;
   - getting expensive, but still worth considering;
   - too expensive to consider.
4. Competitors' published prices (desk research), cited with the date checked.
