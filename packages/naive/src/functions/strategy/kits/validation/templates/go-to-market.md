# Template: the go-to-market record

Fills `<venture>/purpose/validation/go-to-market.json`. The `path` changes what validation weighs (`weights.json`):

| Path | When | Validation weighs more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `build-first` | Ship, then sell | Product-market fit |
| `market-first` | Market the core product before the flagship ships: waitlists, pre-orders | Go-to-market; product-market fit has no floor yet |
| `cofounder-first` | Form the team before the product | Team; product-market fit and pricing weigh less |

For each channel, record `kind` and, once known, `cost_per_customer` and `conversion`. Evidence: customers acquired through the channel (behavior), sign-ups or deposits from it (commitment), outreach response rates (commitment when a meeting is booked).
