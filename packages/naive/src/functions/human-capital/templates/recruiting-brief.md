# Template: recruiting brief

One brief per open person role in `operations/team-plan.json` (`recruiting[].brief`). An agent drafts it; the person who decides on the role approves it.

## The role

- **Role and kind:** cofounder, hire, contractor, advisor or investor.
- **Why a person:** the tasks in the team plan that agents cannot do, may not do, or are not known to do (the capability register), and the decisions this person will hold.
- **Needed by:** the phase, and what happens if the role stays open.

## What they bring

| Skill or access | Why the venture needs it now | Must have or nice to have |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | | |

From the validation team record's gaps. For a cofounder, also: the commitment expected, and the questions the governance kit's `cofounder-structure` skill will raise (roles, vesting, IP, departure).

## What they get

Pay, equity range (from `governance/ownership.json` and the option pool), hours, place.

## Where to find them and how to screen

- **Channels:** where people like this are found.
- **Screen:** the evidence of the skill you will ask for: work shipped, references, a paid trial task.
- **Agent's part:** drafting, searching public profiles, scheduling and first-pass screening against the must-haves. A person makes contact and decides.
