# Acme Labs

A software startup in discovery: an app that watches what an independent cafe sells and drafts the supplier reorder for the owner to approve. It is Naive's demo venture (EE-082, EE-083), created with create-venture, and every check passes on it.

- **People:** Maya Chen, solo founder, wears every hat (`people/`). Maya is fictional.
- **Sources of truth:** the product is pointed at a local repo, GitHub and Vercel; marketing and sales at Vercel's analytics; finance is connected to Rho (`knowledge/sources.json`).
- **Money:** `money/` holds Rho's sandbox, a fictional company Rho runs for developers (Acme Corporation), synced from a saved answer (`platform/integrations/fixtures/rho-sandbox.json`). `knowledge/sources.json` marks it as a fixture. It is not a real account.
- **Decided so far:** a prototype exists, the founder is solo, the problem, the ideal customer, and the goal for discovery (`decisions/`). The vision, the core product and the rest of the foundation are next.
