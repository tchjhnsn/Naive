# Pilot, July to October 2026 (example)

Fictional. Eleven shops from the problem interviews ran the shop app in a paid pilot at $49 a month. Weekly active shops by week are in purpose/validation/product-market-fit.json. In October, 34 active users across the shops answered the Sean Ellis survey; 16 said they would be very disappointed without the app. Two shops cancelled over price, and both were one-person shops outside the segment.
