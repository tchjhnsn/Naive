# Template: the solution record

Fills `<venture>/purpose/validation/solution.json`. Weak: a description of the product. Strong: evidence that people in the segment choose it over what they use now.

| Field | Write |
| --- | --- |
| `statement` | How it solves the problem from `problem.json`, in one or two sentences |
| `alternatives` | What people use instead today, including doing nothing |
| `difference` | Why someone would switch |
| `evidence[]` | Best: people stop using their alternative (behavior). Next: they sign up or pay for a trial (commitment). Weakest: they say they would switch (stated) |
