# Template: problem interview

Gathers `stated` and `behavior` evidence for the problem record. Method: Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test* (references/references.json, `fitzpatrick-2013-mom-test`).

## Before

- Recruit from the target `segment`, not friends or peers. Evidence from the wrong audience drops two grades.
- Plan for at least five interviews; fewer drops one grade.
- Write down the three things you most need to learn.

## Ask (past facts, not opinions about the future)

1. Walk me through the last time this happened. When was it?
2. What did you do about it? What did that cost you, in time or money?
3. What else have you tried? What do you pay for today?
4. Who else is involved when it happens?
5. What would you lose if you stopped dealing with it?

## Do not ask

- "Would you use a product that...?" (hypothetical: general, low signal)
- "Do you like the idea?" (compliments are opinion: very low)

## After

- Save notes in the venture folder (e.g. `knowledge/research/problem-interviews-<date>.md`) and cite the file as `source.path`.
- Record each finding as an evidence item: past spending or workarounds as `behavior`, what they described as `stated` with `specificity: specific`, a booked follow-up or intro as `commitment`.
- Record evidence against the claim too, with `supports: false`.
