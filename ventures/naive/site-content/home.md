# If you have an idea, you are a founder.

Naive helps solo founders and small teams navigate the venture development lifecycle.

## Who it's for
- Technical solo founders who already work in an agent harness (Claude Code, Cursor)

## The problem
Starting a venture is easy now; choosing what to do next is where ideas stall, and a solo founder's knowledge is scattered across tools that agents cannot read as one picture.

## How it works
A venture kept as plain records, with the decision that needs a person put first. Agents propose, and people decide.

## What works today
- Create a venture as plain records (`naive new`)
- Decision docket: what a person must decide next, ranked
- Agents propose; strategy and public-commitment writes become drafts for a person
- Provenance chain on every write
- A source and its text written together (EE-192)
- Lean Canvas view over records

## Next step
One primary action, 'Start your venture', into Mentura account creation, with the plugin install as the secondary link for terminal users. Retire the waitlist once sign-up works end to end.
