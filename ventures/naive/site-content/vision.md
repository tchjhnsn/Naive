# Vision

PROPOSED (Torian, t23u, paraphrased): become the default platform on which small businesses choose to operate and scale.

PROPOSED: a founder with an idea can sign up, run the venture's records, decisions and agents in one place, and keep running it there as it grows. Earlier recorded end: 'more people realize that they are founders' (preserve/merged-ledger.public.md N-197, box copy).

## How we'll know
- Ventures run on Naive (mode run) that had a person decide at least one decision in the last 30 days
- venture:naive itself is run on Naive, and its public site is generated from its records
- Small businesses (not only software startups) running on Naive

## What we're testing now
- Founders value checked definitions (the term engine and Lean) enough that it should be marketed, not only used inside. (open)
- The term engine is needed at all: without it, definition drift would cause wrong agent writes that tests would not catch. (open)
- Early users will run agents on their own AI subscription (Claude Code, MCP) rather than buy credits from us. (open)
- Mentura users without an AI subscription will pay a monthly plan that includes model credits. (open)
- Non-technical small business owners (e.g. online fitness coaching) will run their business's decisions in Mentura weekly. (open)
- AI-native solo founders adopt the plugin and keep using it after the first week. (open)
- Venture studios will pay to run several ventures under one method. (open)
- A done-with-you venture setup service is a faster first revenue than software. (open)
- Founders will buy or share templates, kits and playbooks built on Naive. (open)
- Pooled, anonymised benchmarks from run ventures become a product investors want. (open)
- Licensing observed-venture profiles outward is a viable line (the PitchBook model). (open)
- Others will build on Naive (kits, connectors, frameworks), the Platform phase gate. (open)
- Agents that buy and sell for a venture (agent payment protocols) become a capability small businesses expect within the planning horizon. (open)
- Small business programs and partners (studios, accelerators, local business centres) are a cheaper channel than direct. (open)
- A phase belongs to each premise or function, not only to the whole venture, because real ventures loop back. (open)
- A site generated from the venture's own records, with a working sign-up, persuades partners more than a deck. (open)
