# Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE)

> **Purpose:** Embeds with customers or high-stakes operators to make the product succeed in their context — integration, trust, judgment calls, feedback into the product org. Canonical example of a seat that is usually **`human-required`**.

## Primary pillar

- **Product** (delivery + learning loop). Secondary **Business** (account success, commercial trust).

## Typical responsibilities

- On-site or highly contextual customer engineering
- Translate field constraints into product/engineering backlog signal
- Own outcomes for named accounts or deployments (via position **assignments**)
- Escalate architecture only within agreed authority — does not replace CTO

## Staffing posture (default)

- **`human-required`** — customer-facing trust, liability, and situated judgment
- Supporting work (log summarization, checklist prep, draft runbooks) may be `agent-capable` as *assistive* duties under hybrid occupancy — not as a full FDE substitute

**Staffing rationale (default):** Presence and accountability in the customer’s environment cannot be delegated to an unattended agent without breaking the role.

## Position instances

Per [[position-schema]]. Assignments should name accounts, deployments, or projects — not “all products.”
