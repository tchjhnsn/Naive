# MAD/SAD research template (approved 2026-10-17)

The template for a research item in the MAD/SAD method. Copy the block in "The template" below into a new note, fill the front half before any research starts, and fill the ending at the end. It sits under [[RESEARCH_METHOD]] (Mimetic, Anti-Mimetic, Synthesised-Mimetic); it does not replace that method. The item definition it relies on is in Part 5 of [[MADSAD_CALENDAR_METHODOLOGY_RESEARCH_2026-10-16]] (§47). The first note written to this shape is [[MADSAD_GITHUB_PROJECTS_RESEARCH_2026-10-17]].

## Rules

1. **Research is done when the decision record is filled in, not when the report is written.**
2. **An open research item past its appetite with no decision shows on the calendar as "decision owed".**
3. **An idea dump is raw input.** It becomes research or a handoff only once it has an objective.
4. **Research is a work item when it owes at least one founder decision.**

## The template

### Front half (filled before any research starts)

| Field | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **Why** | One or two sentences. |
| **Objective** | What we want to be able to do afterward. |
| **Source** | The idea dump, handoff or goal it came from. |
| **Questions** | Maximum five. |
| **Decisions owed** | Each stated as a choice with options. The owner decides all of them. |
| **What would change the answer** | The facts or limits that would reverse the recommendation. |
| **Non-goals** | What this research will not do. |
| **Sources allowed** | Primary only, or the named exceptions. |
| **Appetite** | A time limit set before starting. At the limit, stop and report. |
| **Output** | The vault location, and what the final report must contain. |

### Ending (filled at the end)

| Field | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **Findings** | Maximum ten lines. Each claim is labelled verified, secondary or judgment. |
| **Recommendation** | Per decision owed: what, and why. |
| **Decision record** | The owner's answer, the date and the reason. The result is **decided**, **postponed** or **rejected**. |
| **Rolled into** | The work item, goal or handoff created, or "none". |

## Notes

- Labels for claims: verified (the source was read), secondary (a snippet, synthesis or unread page), judgment (my inference). The longer notes also use "unverified" for anything not examined.
- The earlier note [[MADSAD_GITHUB_PROJECTS_RESEARCH_2026-10-17]] used a longer form (a findings section per question, a synthesis, and a decision table). This template caps findings at ten lines; the earlier note predates that cap.
